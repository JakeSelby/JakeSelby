# Jake Selby

I'm a dad, builder, and VP of Engineering at Arcadia, based in Hyattsville, Maryland. I lead engineering teams during the day and build things I care about at night.

My path into tech started in political organizing: field work on the Obama campaign, then data engineering at the DNC. I joined CareJourney as employee #7 and led engineering through its acquisition by Arcadia. That experience still shapes what I want to build: healthcare that reaches people, organizing tools more campaigns can afford, and AI that earns our trust.

## What I'm building

- **[Agent Harness](https://github.com/JakeSelby/agent-harness)** — reusable rules, skills, and hooks for coding agents. My main open-source project. [Browse the reference](https://agent-harness.jakeselby.com/).
- **[Cortex](https://cortex.jakeselby.com/pitch/builders) and [Consul](https://consul.jakeselby.com/pitch)** — exploring how AI can remember what matters, follow your rules, and earn the right to act on your behalf; with a personal assistant built on that foundation.
- **[Groundwork](https://groundwork.jakeselby.com/demo)** — field-organizing tools for progressive campaigns, including canvassing and offline mobile workflows.
- **[Fable](https://fable.jakeselby.com/)** — a date-night companion that helps busy couples find things they'd both enjoy.

I also contribute to **[Parsons](https://github.com/move-coop/parsons)**, the Python toolkit for civic and political data work.

Away from the keyboard: family, Ravens football, guitar and drums, and getting outside.
